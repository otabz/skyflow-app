## What SkyFlow accesses

- **Location.** SkyFlow uses your device's location to fetch current weather for
  your area. If you prefer not to grant location access, you can select a city
  manually instead.
- **Network.** SkyFlow contacts a weather service to retrieve current conditions
  for your location or chosen city.

## What SkyFlow collects and stores

- SkyFlow does not require an account and does not ask for personal information.
- Location is used to request weather and is not sold.
- Your selected city and display preferences are stored locally on your device.

## Third-party services

SkyFlow retrieves weather data from a third-party weather provider. Requests to
that provider include the information needed to return local conditions (such as
coordinates or a city name).
